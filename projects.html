Projects That Create Change

Every project represents a step towards a better society. Through education, empowerment, welfare and environmental activities, we work towards creating meaningful change.

WHAT WE DO

Six Projects, One Mission

InAmigos Foundation works in different areas of social welfare through six major initiatives. Each project focuses on a particular social cause and encourages people to participate in creating positive change.

01 / FOOD & WELFARE
SERVING HUMANITY

Project SEVA

Serving Humanity with Compassion

Project SEVA focuses on supporting underprivileged communities by helping people meet their basic needs. The initiative works towards providing food and clothing to people who need assistance.

Key Activities

  • Food distribution for people in need.
  • Distribution of clothes to underprivileged communities.
  • Encouraging community participation in social welfare.
02 / EDUCATION
BUILDING BRIGHT FUTURES

Project BACHPANSHALA

Nurturing Young Minds, Building Bright Futures

Project BACHPANSHALA focuses on improving educational opportunities for underprivileged children. It aims to support children's learning and help them develop knowledge and essential life skills.

Key Activities

  • Supporting children's school education.
  • Promoting basic digital literacy.
  • Developing essential life skills.
  • Encouraging learning and educational awareness.
03 / ANIMAL WELFARE
CARE FOR EVERY LIFE

Project JEEV

Empowering Lives, Spreading Compassion

Project JEEV is dedicated to animal welfare. It focuses on helping stray and needy animals through feeding, rescue and protection activities. The project encourages kindness towards animals and responsible community participation.

Key Activities

  • Feeding stray animals.
  • Supporting animal rescue activities.
  • Promoting animal protection and welfare.
  • Creating awareness about kindness towards animals.
04 / WOMEN EMPOWERMENT
EMPOWERING WOMEN

Project UDAAN

Soaring Towards a Brighter Future

Project UDAAN works towards women's empowerment by encouraging skill development and financial independence. The foundation collaborates with self-help groups in rural areas to support women's development and awareness.

Key Activities

  • Skill development opportunities for women.
  • Collaboration with rural self-help groups.
  • Promoting financial independence.
  • Menstrual hygiene awareness activities.
05 / ENVIRONMENT
PROTECTING OUR PLANET

Project PRAKRITI

Plant for a Better Tomorrow

Project PRAKRITI focuses on environmental conservation and sustainability. It encourages people to protect nature through plantation activities, environmental awareness and eco-friendly agricultural practices.

Key Activities

  • Tree plantation activities.
  • Environmental awareness campaigns.
  • Promoting sustainable practices.
  • Supporting eco-friendly agriculture.
06 / YOUTH DEVELOPMENT
BUILDING FUTURE SKILLS

Project VIKAS

Developing Skills, Creating Opportunities

Project VIKAS focuses on developing the professional skills of young people through internships and learning opportunities. It helps participants gain practical exposure and improve their employability.

Key Activities

  • Internship opportunities for students and youth.
  • Data operations and finance-related learning.
  • Content writing and digital marketing skills.
  • Research and social work exposure.
